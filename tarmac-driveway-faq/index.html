Tarmac Driveway FAQ

You’re thinking of a tarmac driveway, you probably have a few questions. Tarmac is popular in Ireland because it’s durable and cost effective but you need to know the benefits, costs and maintenance requirements. Below we’ve answered some of the most common questions to help you decide if a tarmac driveway is for you.

How Much Does Tarmac Cost in Ireland?

The cost of tarmac in Ireland can vary depending on size of driveway, preparation work required and type of tarmac used. On average a tarmac driveway will cost between €50 to €100 per square meter. For a precised cost evaluation please use our handy Tarmac Cost Calculator.

Is Tarmac Cheaper Than Block Paving?

Yes tarmac is cheaper than block paving. Block paving can look more decorative and high end but it comes with a higher price tag due to material costs and the labour involved in installation. Tarmac is quicker and easier to install and is a more budget friendly option without compromising on durability.

What Are the Disadvantages of Tarmac?

While tarmac has many benefits, there are a few downsides to consider. For example, tarmac can soften in extreme heat. But this isn’t usually a concern in Ireland’s mild climate. It may also be prone to marking under heavy loads or sharp objects and can develop small cracks over time, especially in areas with freeze-thaw cycles. However, with regular maintenance, tarmac’s durability, affordability, and smooth finish far outweigh these minor drawbacks, making it an excellent choice for a long-lasting and visually appealing driveway in Ireland.

How Long Will a Tarmac Drive Last?

A well installed and maintained tarmac driveway will last 10 to 20 years. Regular maintenance such as cleaning and repairing minor cracks will extend its life. The quality of installation, local weather and the amount of traffic it gets will all have a bearing on how long it will last

How Do I Get Rid of Moss on Tarmac?

Moss on tarmac can be managed by cleaning the surface regularly and using a moss killer if necessary. Apply a moss remover and lightly scrub the affected area and it will be gone. Make sure to use products safe for tarmac surfaces and follow the instructions. 

All the solutions to get rid off moss on a tarmac driveway are detaialed here : How To Remove Moss From Tarmac Driveways

How Thick Is Tarmac on a Driveway?

The thickness of tarmac on a driveway is usually 25mm to 50mm. For residential driveways 25mm to 40mm is sufficient, for heavy duty or commercial areas 40mm to 50mm may be required. The thickness will depend on the load bearing requirements and the sub base beneath the tarmac.

How Do I Clean Oil Off a Tarmac Driveway?

To clean oil from a tarmac driveway start by blotting up any excess oil with a cloth. Then apply an oil remover or household detergent to the stain and scrub the area with a stiff brush. Let it sit for a few minutes before rinsing with water. Avoid using harsh chemicals that can damage the tarmac surface.

How Do I Repair a Tarmac Driveway?

Small cracks in a tarmac driveway can be repaired with a cold lay tarmac repair product available at most hardware stores. For larger areas of damage it’s best to call a professional as they may need to resurface or replace sections of the tarmac to get a long lasting repair.

To know more about this please read our article How to Repair a Tarmac Driveway – A Step-by-Step Guide

Can Tarmac Be Installed Over an Existing Driveway?

In many cases, yes, tarmac can be laid over an existing driveway, provided the base is stable and in good condition. This can save time and reduce costs compared to a full removal. However, a proper assessment is always needed to make sure the existing surface will support the new tarmac and deliver a long-lasting finish.

Does a Tarmac Driveway Add Value to My Home?

A well-laid tarmac driveway can definitely add value to your property. It improves kerb appeal, creates a clean and organised entrance, and shows that the exterior has been properly looked after. For buyers, a neat, low-maintenance driveway is a strong plus and can positively influence how the home is perceived from the very first visit.

Is Tarmac Suitable for Ireland’s Weather?

Yes, tarmac is very well suited to Ireland’s climate. It handles rain, cold temperatures and occasional frost without issue when installed properly. Because it is flexible, it copes better with temperature changes than some rigid surfaces, which helps reduce cracking over time. This makes it a popular and reliable choice for driveways in Dublin and surrounding areas.
