Uses of Asphalt Driveways Dublin

Asphalt is most commonly used for driveways, car parks, and roads, but its versatility goes far beyond that. It can also be used to create a wide range of durable surfaces, from airport runways and racetracks to tennis courts, schoolyards, and cycle paths.

You’ll also find asphalt in more functional settings, such as industrial floors in worksites or greenhouses, as well as on bridges, ramps, and even around rail infrastructure. In short, it’s a highly adaptable material suitable for both residential and large-scale commercial projects.

The Types of Asphalt For Driveways

There are five main types of asphalt, but choosing the right one for your driveway or private road is not always straightforward. Each option has its own strengths, so it is worth taking a closer look to see which one best suits your needs.

SMA (Stone-Matrix Asphalt)

The Stone-matrix asphalt material is made using a blend of asphalt cement, stabilizing agents, and also crushed stone aggregate. It is a durable and hardy kind of asphalt, developed to keep road structure without cracking.

It is most often utilised for roads that get plenty of traffic and would experience plenty of wear & tear with time. Whereas it may not be perfect for just a residential road, it will be a great option for a parking garage or driveway in any industrial property.

All About Driveway Cleaning

Perpetual Pavement

Numerous coatings of asphalt are applied to make a perpetual pavement. They’re stronger as well as can hold a massive load of traffic on them. The base coating is flexible as well as absorbs the maximum of the wear & tear, while the mid coating is applied for strength.

The top coating is of asphalt mixture, which bears the traffic and is smoother. This perpetual pavement is durable, long-lasting, and smooth. Another positive point of perpetual pavement is that it’s easy to maintain and quick to complete the paving work.

Porous Asphalt

The Porous asphalt material is thought to be the best for residential properties and homes. A thin coating of asphalt is applied over aggregate materials and thus allows dampness to be soaked into the surface.

This has outstanding drainage properties. In case that’s what you’re searching for, then the porous asphalt material is the best choice for you.

Quiet Pavement

This kind of pavement is perfect for places that get plenty of traffic. But plenty of traffic means plenty of noise for nearby residential or commercial properties. This type of paving cuts down noise pollution by absorbing the jigglings made by such traffic.

Although you might think it isn’t good for a driveway, think how noisy a park or an apartment’s entrance will be with the vehicles of residents driving near it all day long!

Thin Asphalt Overlay

In case you have already got a driveway, you can use a thin layer of asphalt overlay on the top of the existing driveway to cover its structural goodness. It even restores the driveway’s smoothness while hiding negligible cracks.

The thin asphalt overlays are normally 3.8 cm or less. Thus, you can even maintain the driveway’s slope with minimal drainage.

Choosing an Asphalt Contractor in Dublin

Asphalt driveways, tarmac driveways, concrete driveways, gravel driveways, and block paving are usually installed by experienced contractors in Dublin, as the work requires specialist equipment, proper preparation, and the right installation methods.

Before choosing a contractor, it is worth doing a bit of research. Not every company delivers the same standard of work, and hiring the wrong one can leave you with poor workmanship, unnecessary costs, and a driveway that will not last as it should.

When speaking to driveway contractors, ask what materials they use for the base and how they prepare and compact it. A strong foundation is essential for a durable asphalt driveway. If you are replacing an old driveway, it is also worth asking whether the existing base will be removed, repaired, or improved before the new asphalt is laid.

You should also check that the contractor uses the proper equipment for the job, including a suitable roller to compact the asphalt evenly and create a solid, long-lasting finish.

Final Thoughts

If you are looking for a driveway surface that is durable, cost-effective, and practical, asphalt is a strong option to consider. While tarmac driveways can sometimes cost slightly less upfront, asphalt often offers better long-term value thanks to its flexibility, smooth finish, and overall performance.

As this article has shown, there are several types of asphalt, and each one is suited to different needs. 

The right choice will depend on how the surface will be used, the level of traffic it will need to handle, and the features that matter most to you. By understanding the different options available, you can choose the asphalt surface that best fits your project.
