5 Tarmac Driveway Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

When it comes to tarmac driveways, detail is everything. Even small mistakes during installation or maintenance can become big problems down the line and affect the durability and appearance of the driveway. In this article we’ll look at 5 common mistakes to avoid and how to make sure your tarmac driveway lasts the test of time.

Mistake 1: Not Preparing the Driveway Base Properly

One of the most important parts of tarmac driveway installation is preparing a solid and level base. Not preparing the base properly can lead to uneven settlement, cracks and costly repairs. Using crushed stone and gravel for the base, compacted with professional equipment ensures stability. An uncompact base is often the root of premature cracking and settlement, especially under heavy vehicles which can reduce the life of the driveway.

Tip: Get experienced contractors to prepare the base for you. This will save you from costly repairs down the line.

Mistake 2: Choosing the Wrong Tarmac Thickness

The thickness of the tarmac layer is key to the driveway’s load-bearing capacity. Residential driveways may require a different thickness than car parks or commercial areas that get heavy vehicles daily. Installing a new asphalt driveway with the wrong thickness can lead to premature wear and tear, especially under extreme weather conditions.

Tip: For residential tarmac driveways 2-3 inches is often enough, for commercial spaces up to 4 inches.

Mistake 3: Poor Drainage

In Ireland’s wet climate proper drainage is crucial. When water sits on top of the asphalt driveway it can seep into the material cause cracks and create a perfect environment for potholes to form. Without drainage water can also weaken the base of the driveway and cause further damage over time.

Tip: Make sure the driveway has a slight slope to allow water to run off. Installing proper drains will prevent water from pooling on the surface and protect your driveway.

Mistake 4: Using Low Grade Tarmac or Materials

Using low grade materials may save you money in the short term but the long term costs will far outweigh the savings. Low grade materials are more prone to damage from UV rays, air bubbles and air voids which can cause premature cracking and premature wear. High-grade asphalt mix that meets industry standards will provide better resistance to extreme weather, heavy loads and frequent use.

Tip: Investing in high-grade materials and hiring a professional asphalt contractor will ensure your tarmac driveway will last longer and require fewer repairs over time.

Mistake 5: Not Maintaining Regularly

Once the driveway is installed you must maintain it. Many people neglect basic maintenance tasks like sealing which protects the asphalt surface from UV rays, water and oil spills. Without a seal, coat asphalt driveways are more prone to cracking and wear and tear. Regular cleaning to remove debris and fill in small cracks will prevent them from becoming bigger problems. Tip: Seal every few years, especially in areas with harsh winters or heavy traffic. A regular maintenance routine of sweeping and occasional re-sealing will extend the life of the driveway.

How long will a Tarmac Driveway Last?

A well-installed and maintained tarmac driveway will last between 10-20 years depending on the grade of materials used and the level of maintenance. Extreme weather, heavy vehicles and frequent traffic will reduce the life expectancy of the driveway.

How long does Tarmac take to fully cure?

The tarmac will take around 24-48 hours to cure enough for light traffic. Full curing where the surface is fully hardened will take several months. During this period you must not park heavy vehicles on the surface and minimise sharp turns to prevent indentations and cracks.

Conclusion

By avoiding these common asphalt paving mistakes and investing in high-grade materials and proper asphalt driveway installation, you can ensure that your tarmac driveway will be durable and visually appealing. Regular maintenance, such as sealing and cleaning, is key to preserving the asphalt surface and preventing costly repairs. For Dublin homeowners, working with experienced professionals familiar with the Irish climate will give you a newly installed asphalt driveway that is not only more stable and longer-lasting but also adds value to your property. Prioritising asphalt paving done right means a driveway you can rely on for years to come.
