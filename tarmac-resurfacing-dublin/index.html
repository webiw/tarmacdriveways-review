DO YOU NEED RESURFACING?

When Tarmac Resurfacing Makes Sense

Resurfacing works best when the existing driveway remains structurally sound but the surface itself has deteriorated.

  • Faded or weathered tarmac
  • Light surface cracking
  • Small potholes or damaged patches
  • Rough or worn areas
  • An old driveway that looks tired or patchy
  • A sound existing base that can be retained
Not sure? We can inspect the existing surface and advise whether resurfacing, repair or full replacement is the better option.

HOW WE WORK

Our Tarmac Resurfacing Process

Good resurfacing starts with the condition of the existing surface, not simply with laying fresh tarmac over the top.

1

Site Inspection

We assess the existing surface, base, levels, edges and drainage.

2

Preparation

Loose material, vegetation and debris are removed and the existing surface is prepared.

3

Repairs

Weak areas, potholes or sunken sections are addressed where required before resurfacing.

4

Drainage & Levels

Existing falls and drainage are checked so that water can run away correctly.

5

New Surface

The new tarmac or asphalt layer is laid, levelled and compacted to create the finished surface.

FAQ

Tarmac Resurfacing Questions

Can an old tarmac driveway be resurfaced?

Yes, if the existing driveway and base remain suitable. We first assess the condition of the surface, drainage and underlying structure before recommending resurfacing.

Is resurfacing cheaper than replacing a driveway?

Resurfacing can require less excavation and groundwork than a complete replacement when the existing base can be retained. The final cost depends on the condition and size of the project.

Can you resurface an asphalt driveway?

Yes. Existing asphalt surfaces can often be resurfaced provided they are stable and correctly prepared beforehand.

Do you resurface commercial car parks?

Yes. We undertake tarmac and asphalt resurfacing for commercial entrances, car parks, yards and access areas as well as domestic driveways.

Do you provide resurfacing outside Dublin?

Yes. We work throughout Dublin, Kildare and surrounding areas.
