Can you resurface an existing driveway in Ballyfermot?

Yes, where the existing driveway and base remain suitable. We assess the condition of the surface, levels, drainage and underlying structure before recommending resurfacing.

The most suitable surface depends on the condition of the site, expected traffic, drainage, budget and the appearance you want. We can assess the property and explain which options are suitable before you decide.
