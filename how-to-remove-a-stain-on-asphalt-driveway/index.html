What Is An Asphalt Driveway?

Asphalt is a mixture of bitumen, gravel, and sand with high porosity. Bitumen provides fast curing and compaction of asphalt, forming a smooth surface with a grey tint. Asphalt driveway material is quite similar to road crews applying on a new driveway. Also known as hot asphalt or hot mix asphalt, this style of driveway is a mixture of crushed stone and sand, with a sludge of asphalt and a tar-like material produced from petroleum. And thus, Asphalt delivers a smoother surface and better finish than the tarmac driveways. Asphalt pavement is laid where vehicles move – on highways, airfields, gas stations, parking lots, service stations, etc.

Asphalt is easy to install material to use both on new or existing surface. These also require less maintenance, these are cost effective, weather resistant, can handle heavy traffic, consists of larger aggregates, etc that makes asphalt the best material for caravan parks, driveways, asphalt paving, etc. However, no matter how low maintenance asphalt is, removing oil stain from asphalt driveways are way more harder than an installation which require expert’s assistance.

Why Do Oil Stains Appear On Asphalt Driveways/Tarmac Driveways?

It’s not surprising that drops of engine oil, which lubricate the parts and mechanisms of the car, spray out from the exhaust pipe or are released from the mechanism during technical breakdowns. Often on asphalt private roads there are stains from machine oils that fall on asphalt pavements from car engines and other mechanisms. There are cases of accidental oil spills at service stations during replacement. Improvised tools will not help in cleaning large areas: gas stations, parking areas, highways and runways at airfields. Oil spills spoil the appearance of new asphalt driveways and the ecological background, pollute the environment and violate fire safety. Why Should You Take Immediated Action To Remove Oil Stains From Asphalt Paving

If at least a small spot of oil appears at the place where the car stopped, this is already a distress signal for the car. But how to remove oil stains from asphalt if they appear again and again. After the asphalt-bitumen mass has dried, new roads and sites look attractive, clean, and have a rich dark gray coloured tarmac. But if even a drop of oil falls on this beauty, the spots spread over a large area, requiring an immediate solution to the problem of how to remove oil stains from asphalt. Asphalt is a rather porous material, although it contains a lot of resin. Oil, falling on the asphalt, gradually penetrates deep into the lower layers. The best way is to clean up the oil as soon as it spills. To do this, you can take dry sand, and cement to spread on the stain. The dry substance quickly absorbs the oil and it remains only to dispose of the residue.

How To Remove Oil Stains From Asphalt If They've Already Been Absorbed?

It’s possible to remove only the layer remaining on the surface mechanically, otherwise the asphalt laying will be disturbed. You may try to wash off oil stains using a solvent, but you will not get a sufficient effect. The solvent doesn’t help to remove the oil completely. You have to turn to new technologies. Chemical manufacturers offer a cleaner that breaks down only the oil without affecting the constituents of the asphalt. After applying the finished solution to the oil stain, a chemical reaction occurs without an aggressive effect on the asphalt. Then you just have to wash off the asphalt with plenty of water and get an excellent result! Expert Advice On Removing Car Oil Stain On Asphalt

Since asphalt is a rather porous surface, it is best to remove the oil stain as soon as it appears. Experts know that fresh oil of natural or synthetic origin has a liquid structure. Spilling onto the asphalt, it forms a puddle. In this case, you need to show skill and quickly cover the oil stain with a dry substance: sand, cement, caustic soda. The oil is absorbed, then the dirty slurry must be cleaned off the asphalt. For a full effect, the place of contamination can be washed with water under pressure. If at first it is imperceptible, in large quantities it completely pollutes the surface. For washing in such cases, use detergents that break down fats. If the surface is small, the work will be successful and the asphalt will again shine like a new one. But for large areas, this method is too laborious and costly. The most successful way to remove an oil stain from asphalt is to use a special chemical agent. The product is concentrated, so it’s suitable for preparing solutions for cleaning large areas.
