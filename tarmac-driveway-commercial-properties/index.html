Tarmac Driveways for Commercial Properties: What You Need to Know

Table of Contents

  2. Why tarmac is the right material for commercial properties
  3. Tarmac Driveways vs Other Materials
  4. Maintenance and long-term care
  5. Get professional tarmac contractors in Dublin

When it comes to commercial properties, especially in busy areas like Dublin, the choice of driveway material matters. The right material will improve your business look and functionality. Tarmac driveways are popular among business owners because they are durable, low maintenance and cost-effective. 

What commercial properties need

Commercial properties like shopping centres, office blocks industrial sites or local businesses have different needs when it comes to their driveways and car parks. The high volume of vehicles, including heavy vehicles, demands a surface that can withstand constant use without compromising on safety or looks. Minimal disruption during the installation process is also crucial so business can carry on as usual.

Why tarmac is the right material for commercial properties

Tarmac driveways are a smart choice for any business, offering a blend of durability, affordability, and style that’s hard to beat.

Let’s dive into why tarmac driveways might be the perfect fit for your commercial property.

1. Durable and Low-Maintenance

One of the biggest benefits of tarmac driveways is their durability. Designed to withstand the high traffic of commercial properties, tarmacadam driveways will last for decades with minimal maintenance. Occasional resurfacing and repairs will keep the surface in top condition, ensuring long-term functionality and a consistent look.

Further reading: How to Repair a Tarmac Driveway – A Step-by-Step Guide

2. Cost-Effective and quick installation

Tarmac driveways are also cost-effective. The initial cost of tarmac is lower than concrete or block paving. Plus, the quick installation process means minimal disruption to your business. A new tarmac driveway installed by an experienced team of contractors means that your business premises will be back up and running in no time.

3. Aesthetic and Property Value

Beyond functionality, tarmac driveways add to the overall look of commercial properties. The smooth surface adds to the appeal, making your business property look better. This aesthetic enhancement will also increase the property value, making tarmac a worthwhile investment.

Tarmac Driveways vs Other Materials

When considering driveway options you need to compare tarmac to other materials like asphalt, concrete and gravel. 

1. Asphalt Driveway

Asphalt and tarmac driveways are often used interchangeably but there is a difference. We explain it all in our article: Key Differences Between Tarmac vs Asphalt Driveways 

2. Concrete Driveway

Concrete is another option, popular for commercial properties. However, the higher initial cost and the tendency to crack under heavy loads make concrete less appealing than tarmac. Concrete driveways can give a different look but require more maintenance and repair, making them less cost-effective in the long run.

3. Gravel Driveway

Gravel is the cheapest option but falls short in many areas compared to tarmac. Gravel driveways require constant maintenance as the gravel can shift and create uneven surfaces. Gravel is also less durable and has lower aesthetic appeal than tarmac or asphalt driveways.

Maintenance and long-term care

Maintaining a tarmac driveway is straightforward, requiring simple steps like regular cleaning to remove debris and prompt repairs of any cracks or holes. Routine maintenance, including sweeping and minor repairs, helps keep the driveway in top condition, while periodic inspections by experts can prevent small issues from becoming major problems.

Over time, repairs like filling cracks or resurfacing with a new tarmac layer can rejuvenate the driveway, restoring its smooth surface and functionality, making tarmac driveways a low-maintenance option compared to other materials.
