Tarmac borders and designs

We offer a huge range of borders, kerbing, walls, flower beds and brick and block paving designs such as circles and diamonds to add a unique element to your tarmac project. There is a misconception that tarmac can be dark and plain, but with a little thought during design, a tarmac driveway can stand out and add an element of class to your property. 

Tarmac can quite easily be coupled with granite cobbles which have long been used and recognised at the entrances of prestigious homes. A border and apron with granite setts, with or without a granite centre piece can make your driveway really stand out. Our borders are available in a range of colours, such as charcoal, grey, natural, rustic, gold, yellow, and more so you are sure to find the right combination to enhance your propertys aesthetics.

 Q. What is the difference between tarmac and asphalt driveways? 













Tarmac mixes tar with aggregate; asphalt mixes bitumen (a petroleum product) with aggregate. Asphalt is laid hotter, cures to a smoother, slightly more flexible surface, and generally handles heavier traffic better, while tarmac has a rougher texture that provides excellent grip and is often more cost-effective for small residential driveways.
 











 Q. How long does a tarmac driveway last? 















With proper installation and routine upkeep (occasional sealing and patch repairs), a tarmac driveway usually lasts 15–20 years, and sometimes up to 25 years in a mild climate like Ireland’s.
