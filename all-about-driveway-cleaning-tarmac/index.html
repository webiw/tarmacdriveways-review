All About Driveway Cleaning - How to Properly Clean and Maintain Your Tarmac Driveway

A clean tarmac driveway not only looks good but also lasts longer. One key part of that is regular driveway cleaning. In this post, we will go through the steps to clean and maintain your tarmac driveway, focusing on the best driveway cleaning methods.

Regular cleaning and maintenance of your tarmac driveway will prolong its life, prevent damage and save you money on repairs. A clean and maintained driveway also looks good to visitors and potential buyers.

Equipment and Materials Needed for Driveway Cleaning

Before you start cleaning your driveway gather the following:

  • Broom or brush
  • Hose with a spray nozzle or pressure washer
  • Mild detergent or tarmac cleaner
  • Stiff-bristled brush or driveway cleaning machine
  • Gloves and eyewear

Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Your Tarmac Driveway

Follow these steps:

a. Clear debris and dirt:

Use a broom or brush to remove loose dirt, leaves, or debris from the driveway’s surface.

b. Apply the cleaning solution:

Mix a mild detergent or tarmac cleaner with water as per the manufacturer’s instructions. Apply to the driveway and make sure to cover evenly.

c. Scrub the driveway:

Use a stiff-bristled brush or a driveway cleaning machine to scrub the surface of the driveway. Focus on stained or dirty areas.

d. Rinse with water:

Once you have scrubbed the driveway, rinse with water using a hose with a spray nozzle or pressure washer. Start at the top of the driveway and work your way down so dirty water doesn’t flow onto the cleaned areas.

e. Allow the driveway to dry:

Let the driveway dry completely before using it again.

Removing Stains and Common Tarmac Driveway Problems

Some problems may need extra treatment:

Oil and grease stains:

a. Apply an oil and grease remover or a mixture of hot water, detergent and baking soda to the area.
b. Scrub the stain with a stiff-bristled brush.
c. Rinse the area thoroughly with water.
d. If the stain persists repeat the process or use a commercial cleaner as per the manufacturer’s instructions.

Moss and algae growth:

a. Moss and algae remover or equal parts water and white vinegar.
b. Spray or apply to the area.
c. Leave for 15-20 minutes to break down the moss and algae.
d. Scrub with a stiff-bristled brush.
e. Rinse thoroughly with water.
f. To prevent regrowth use a moss and algae inhibitor available at garden centres.

How to remove moss from Tarmac Driveways

Paint or chemical spills:

a. For water-based paint spills use a paint or chemical spill remover as per the product instructions or a mixture of warm water and mild detergent.
b. For oil-based paint spills use a paint thinner or specialized paint remover. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions as some chemicals can be harmful to tarmac surfaces.
c. Scrub the area with a stiff-bristled brush.
d. Rinse the area thoroughly with water.
e. If the spill persists repeat the process or seek professional help.

Proper Tarmac Driveway Maintenance

Sealcoating Your Tarmac Driveway

Cleaning your tarmac driveway is important but so is sealcoating. This applies a layer on the surface of the driveway to protect it from UV, water and chemicals. You should seal coat your tarmac driveway every 3 to 5 years depending on usage and climate. Sealcoating not only protects your driveway but also makes it look like new again.

Filling Cracks and Potholes

Cleaning your driveway regularly will help you spot any cracks or potholes early on so you can address them quickly. You should fill cracks and potholes as soon as possible to prevent further damage to your tarmac driveway. There are DIY crack and pothole repair kits available but often it’s best to hire a professional for a long-term solution.

Preventing Damage from Heavy Vehicles or Objects

To keep your tarmac driveway in good condition don’t place heavy vehicles or objects on the surface for extended periods. The weight can cause the tarmac to crack or sink and will cost you more to repair. If you need to park a heavy vehicle on your driveway consider using driveway support grids or reinforced tarmac to minimise the risk of damage.

Regular Inspections and Timely Repairs

In addition to regular cleaning, regular inspections of your tarmac driveway are important to spot any potential issues early on. Look for signs of wear and tear such as cracks, fading or uneven surfaces. Fix these issues as they arise to prevent more damage and extend the life of your driveway.
